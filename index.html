Frequently asked questions

Before you get started

Can I request both electrical work and solar installation?

Yes. Choose “Electrical and solar package” in the inquiry form and describe both requirements.

What information is useful for a solar quotation?

Your recent electricity bill or monthly kWh usage, the appliances you want to run, desired backup time, project location, and photos of the roof or installation area are useful starting points.

Can you provide an exact price immediately?

A reliable quote depends on the site, system size, materials, equipment, and installation scope. We may need additional details or a site assessment before confirming pricing.

Do you serve areas outside Cebu?

Contact us with your location and project details so we can confirm availability and any travel or site-assessment costs.
